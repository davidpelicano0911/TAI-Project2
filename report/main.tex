\begin{abstract}
We present two lossless compressors tailored to raw 16-bit astronomical
images ($1500\times1500$ pixels, big-endian) acquired by ground-based
telescopes. \textbf{prism-block} applies a gradient-adjusted predictor and
a context-adaptive range coder to horizontal image bands, achieving
3.51\,bpp in $\sim$81\,ms per image. \textbf{HAIS} selects the best of five
per-block predictors — including training-free least-squares — and codes
residuals with Finite State Entropy (tANS)~\cite{collet2013fse}, reaching
3.40\,bpp in $\sim$187\,ms. Both codecs outperform the best generic
compressor (zstd-19 at 4.00\,bpp) by at least 0.49\,bpp while remaining
faster than bzip2-9 and xz-9, and they lie on the Pareto frontier of the
full benchmark.
\end{abstract}
