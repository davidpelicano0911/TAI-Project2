\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work confirms the expected trade-off between compression strength
and runtime. ACPAC achieved the best overall compression, LZAC was the
fastest of the proposed codecs, and APEC occupied a useful middle
ground between both extremes. More broadly, the results show that
changing only the predictive model, while keeping arithmetic coding as
the common back-end, is enough to produce clearly different operating
points in practice.
