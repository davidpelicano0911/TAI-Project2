\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work confirms the expected trade-off between compression strength and
runtime. compression-focused achieved the best overall compression ratio
(3.37\,bpp), speed-focused was the fastest of the three codecs (47\,ms
total), and all-rounder occupied a useful middle ground (3.40\,bpp in
93\,ms). More broadly, the results show that specialising the predictive
model for 16-bit astronomical images is enough to outperform all tested
general-purpose compressors in compression ratio while remaining competitive
in runtime.
