\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Compression Ratio}

Table~\ref{tab:bpp} reports bits per byte for each of the eight benchmark
images. All three domain-specific codecs outperform every generic compressor
on every image. compression-focused achieves the best average ratio
(3.37\,bpp), reducing average bpp by $0.21$ versus bzip2-9 and by $0.81$
versus zstd-9. all-rounder follows closely ($+0.03$\,bpp over
compression-focused) and speed-focused is $0.11$\,bpp above all-rounder,
still beating every generic tool.

The margin is consistent across image types. Image~F (science frame with
bright point sources, $H_0 = 12.52$\,bpp) is the hardest for all codecs
because its high raw entropy leaves little room for prediction; even so,
compression-focused saves $0.41$\,bpp over bzip2-9 on that image alone.
Image~G (bias frame, $H_0 = 5.57$\,bpp) is the easiest: the near-constant
DC pedestal is entirely captured by Mode~3 and compressed to 2.38\,bpp,
$0.16$\,bpp below bzip2-9.

\subsection{Speed}

Table~\ref{tab:speed} shows average per-image runtimes.
speed-focused achieves 49\,ms total, only 22\,ms slower than zstd-1 but
with $0.98$\,bpp better compression. all-rounder takes 96\,ms,
$4.5\times$ faster than bzip2-9 (432\,ms) at $0.18$\,bpp better ratio.
compression-focused trades a slow compression pass (${\sim}6.2$\,s) for
the best ratio; decompression (${\sim}133$\,ms) is faster than bzip2-9.
